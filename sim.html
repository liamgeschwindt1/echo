Limits of this simulation

  • Free field plus the ground and any buildings you add. Buildings are axis-aligned concrete boxes with one placeholder absorption (0.03, not measured). Only the vertical faces and the ground reflect; roofs block sound but do not reflect.
  • There is no diffraction. A building between a source and a node removes the direct path completely (a pessimistic worst case), and there is no transmission through walls. Real facades reflect less cleanly than flat boxes.
  • The wave rings are free field and ignore buildings.
  • Uniform air, no wind, static sources.
  • Source levels, ground reflectivity and the SPL-to-dBFS constant are placeholders until confirmed.
  • Ambient noise is independent per mic, which is pessimistic for localisation.
  • Mic timing has no hardware jitter, clock drift or mismatch.
  • A flat-square node cannot tell above from below and assumes the source is below it.
  • Rings show loudness over time, not true wavelength.

Repeats and sweeps

Each seed re-renders every mic, so large runs take minutes. Fix rate is the share of sounds that got a position.
