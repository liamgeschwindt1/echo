# Provisional localisation targets

Median horizontal fix error under 5 m and p90 under 10 m, to 50 m source range, are starting design targets. They are **placeholders**, not validated hardware requirements. Report coverage and wrong fixes alongside errors; no-fix must never count as zero error.

`targets.json` supplies Testing pass/fail colours. Benchmarks spanning farther ranges are not strict target acceptance tests. Hardware requirements cannot be inferred until controlled realism-on sweeps and real-recording calibration support them. No mic self-noise, heading or timing accuracy requirement is claimed yet.
